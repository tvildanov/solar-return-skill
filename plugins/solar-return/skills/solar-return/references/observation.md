# AstroEmpathy: optional observation / добровольное наблюдение

A short adaptation of AstroEmpathy's experiential approach, shared with the author's permission. It documents what a person notices and the meaning they choose to give it; it does not establish a physical influence of celestial bodies or a medical benefit.

## Keep three layers separate

- **Calculated:** the actual script output, time zone, assumptions and version. An observation cannot correct or confirm an astronomical calculation.
- **Symbolic:** optional astrological themes, including the 12-before/12-after calendar. Label them as interpretive prompts, not events that must happen.
- **Observed:** only what the person reports about their body, emotions and attention, in their own words. An image, expectation or interpretation is not the same thing as a bodily sensation. Never infer an unreported experience from the chart.

## A brief, voluntary sequence

1. **Choose.** Ask whether they want an observation exercise at all. A calculation-only report is complete. They choose the duration and may skip any question or stop without explanation; discomfort is a reason to stop, not a sign to continue.
2. **Before.** If they wish, make a brief baseline note before reading the symbolic interpretation. Ask neutrally: “What, if anything, do you notice now?” Optional fields: bodily sensation and location; emotion; quality of attention. “Nothing noticeable”, “unsure” and a blank answer are valid. Do not suggest which sensation or emotion they should find.
3. **Observe.** At a convenient time, optionally repeat the same neutral note. No exact-minute ritual, forced stillness or special feeling is required. Use the calculated return as a calendar reference, not as a claimed cause of sensations. Attention to the Sun means reflecting on the event, **not looking directly at the Sun**.
4. **Compare.** Keep original notes separate from later interpretations. Note ordinary context, if relevant: sleep, activity, stress, surroundings, and whether the interpretation was already read. Keep unchanged experiences and mismatches, not only apparent coincidences. A change, repetition or correlation does not establish celestial causation, validate astrology or diagnose a condition.

An optional entry: **time + zone | before/after reading | body | emotion | attention | everyday context | later reflection**. A person may add a self-chosen intensity scale, but it is subjective, not a calibrated health measure. Missing entries do not mean “no feeling”; skipping a day needs no catch-up and implies no effect on the year.

## Consent and privacy

Keep notes privately unless the person chooses to share them. Do not request sensitive detail or automatically save, upload, publish or merge observations into the birth-data JSON. Explain that notes entered in a hosted chat are processed under that service's policies; do not promise anonymity or confidentiality. Sharing another person's observations requires their consent. This is an optional reflection aid, not treatment, diagnosis or a validated research instrument.

## Коротко по-русски

Разделяйте **расчёт**, **символическую тему** и **личное наблюдение**. По желанию: сначала нейтральная запись о теле, эмоциях и внимании, затем повторная запись в удобное время и спокойное сравнение с учётом обычных обстоятельств. Можно ничего не заметить, не отвечать или остановиться; пропущенную запись нельзя считать отсутствием ощущений. Не подгоняйте опыт под карту и не считайте совпадение доказательством влияния планет. Не смотрите прямо на Солнце. Записи остаются личными, пока человек сам не решит ими поделиться; чат не гарантирует конфиденциальности. Это не диагностика и не лечение.
